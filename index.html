Understanding the issue

What Is Land Subsidence?

Land subsidence is the slow sinking of the ground. It happens gradually, but its effects on homes, roads, and farms can be serious.

1

Water Is Pumped Out

Homes, farms, and fishponds draw groundwater from deep underground layers called aquifers. Heavy use depletes these layers over time.

2

The Ground Compacts

When water is removed, soil and rock layers lose their support. They press together and the surface above sinks slowly, sometimes by centimeters each year.

3

Problems Compound Over Time

Lower ground means worse flooding, cracked buildings, and saltwater entering freshwater sources. The change is gradual but largely irreversible.
